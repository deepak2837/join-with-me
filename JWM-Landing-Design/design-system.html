One design direction — “The Trail” — with three interchangeable palettes. Everything on the landing page is built from the tokens below.

01 · Colour themes

Three palettes, one system

Every theme keeps green as the primary and a yellow as the secondary, as agreed. Swapping a theme changes only the CSS variables — no layout, type or spacing changes. Pick one and the whole page follows.

05 · Rules for whoever builds this

Non-negotiables

These are the decisions that keep the design intact when the copy changes, which the brief specifically asked for.

01

Text length must not break layout. Every headline, card and list item is set in a flexible container with wrapping allowed. Cards stretch to the tallest sibling in the row; nothing relies on a fixed height.

02

Type scales fluidly, never jumps. All sizes use clamp(), so 360px and 1600px both look deliberate. Do not swap in fixed px sizes.

03

Green is primary, yellow is secondary — always. Yellow is an accent only: price badges, stat numbers, the play button, one icon per panel. It never carries body text.

04

The logo is untouched. It sits on a forest-green rounded square at 44px so the lime mark stays legible on the paper background.

05

Photography is real. Every image on the page is from Join With Me's own walks. No stock, no AI imagery. Promo graphics with baked-in text were deliberately replaced with clean photographs so the card titles do the talking.

06

The sticky bar is the safety net. It appears once the hero scrolls past and never leaves. On mobile it replaces the header CTA entirely.

07

Motion is enhancement, not structure. Everything reads correctly with JavaScript off and with prefers-reduced-motion on. Add ?flat=1 to any URL to freeze all animation in its final state.

08

Contrast holds. Body text on paper, white on forest and muted-on-dark all clear WCAG AA at their used sizes.
