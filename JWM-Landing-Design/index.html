04 A day with us

Five hours, start
to finish.

Every Join With Me experience follows the same — arrive, eat, walk, play, eat again, go home tired and happy. Here it is, hour by hour.

8:00 AM

Arrival & breakfast

Start the morning with a simple, hot breakfast at the host house while you meet the rest of the group. No early-morning heroics.

8:30 AM

Introductions & briefing

Your host runs through the route, the pace, the water crossings and the plan for children and pets. Questions welcome.

8:45 AM

The walk begins

An offbeat trail — through paddy, past streams, with plenty of stops to look at whatever your naturalist points out.

9:45 AM

The water stop

A waterfall, a river bend or a forest lake, depending on the walk — always shallow, always safe. Where the children stop wanting to go home.

11:00 AM

Open ground & water play

Games, a swim for those who want one, and an hour of doing absolutely nothing in particular.

12:00 PM

Back for lunch

Return to the host house for a farm-to-table thali cooked by the village — the meal people write to us about afterwards.

1:00 PM

Conclusion

Best Hiker awards for the children, a group photo, and you’re back in Mumbai by early evening.

06 The practical bit

What’s included.
What to carry.

Included in your ticket

One price. Nothing added at the trailhead.

  • A professionally crafted, route-tested hiking experience
  • An adventure host equipped with a first-aid kit
  • Nutritious breakfast and a home-cooked lunch
  • Water time — a lake, river or waterfall stop
  • Access to private, offbeat spots kept for our group
  • Parking, entry fees and all permits

Things to carry

Six items. That is genuinely the whole list.

  • Comfortable shoes — and camouflage-coloured clothes if you have them
  • One litre of water per person
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Dry snacks or energy bars, if you like to graze
  • A cap
  • Any personal medication

10 Questions

Everything people
ask us first.

Yes. Every route is chosen to be beginner-friendly and walkable at a slow pace, with regular stops. If you can walk comfortably for a few hours with breaks, you can do a Join With Me walk. Our hosts set the pace to the slowest person in the group, never the fastest.

Children, parents and grandparents all join the same walks — that is the point of them. Younger children usually manage the full route with a carry or two from a parent. If you’re bringing someone very young or very senior, message us before booking and we’ll tell you honestly which walk suits them best.

Many of our experiences are pet-friendly, including the Lakeside and Riverside walks. Dogs need to be comfortable around children and other dogs. Confirm with us when you book so we can plan the group.

Rain is usually the best part — the monsoon is when the waterfalls run and the paddy fields flood. Walks go ahead in normal rain. We only reschedule for genuinely unsafe weather, and we let you know as early as we can.

Local train is the most budget-friendly option, and autos and cabs are readily available from the station to the base village. We share the exact location a day before your walk, along with a suggested train schedule. You can also drive — parking is included.

Breakfast and lunch are home-cooked by the host village and are vegetarian by default. Tell us about allergies or dietary needs when you book and we’ll pass it on to the kitchen.

Yes. We run private hikes from ₹999 per person for birthdays, school outings, corporate teams and family groups, on a date you choose. Call or WhatsApp us and we’ll build it around your group.

Cancellations and transfers are covered by our refund policy. If your plans change, message us as early as possible — we will always try to move you to another date first.
