# Client message — design handover

*Short format. Swap the bracketed bits, cut anything that doesn't sound like you.*

---

Hi [Name],

The Join With Me website design is ready, and we're excited to share it with you.

We've designed the complete landing page — from the hero right through to the footer — and built it in **three colour themes** so you can see the brand in three moods and choose, rather than approve something in the abstract.

**On the colours.** Our research kept pointing the same way: for a brand that is fundamentally about nature, a natural green reads as the primary colour — it carries calm, trust and the outdoors instantly, before a single word is read. Working from that and from your own logo palette, these three came out as the strongest options:

- **Sahyadri** — deep forest green with the lime yellow from your logo
- **Monsoon** — river teal-green with a warm golden yellow
- **Terracotta Trail** — farm olive with ochre

Every palette is contrast-checked so the text stays readable on a bright phone screen outdoors and over photographs. If you have another colour direction in mind, tell us — we're glad to explore it.

**A few things we're especially proud of:**

- The **reels transition** — your Instagram films come forward one by one as you scroll, like a deck being dealt
- The **itinerary transition** — the day's plan walks sideways to the right as you scroll down, hour by hour, and holds still until you've seen the whole day
- We've built **a lot of visuals out of your own content** — your photographs and films from the walks are doing the heavy lifting throughout, not stock imagery
- Designed for the phone first, since that's where most of your visitors will find you

**How to review it.** You'll find:

- Full-page screenshots — the whole page in one image, for each theme, desktop and mobile
- Section-by-section images — so you can give us precise notes ("section 06, second card" is all we need)
- A screen recording — a real scroll through the site on a phone, so you can see how it actually moves

We've made every section the best we could imagine it, and put our full creativity into giving you something exclusive — a site that looks like nobody else's, because it's built from your walks and your people.

Any changes are welcome, big or small. Do let us know your feedback, and which theme you'd like us to take forward.

Warm regards,
[Your name]
