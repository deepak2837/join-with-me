🌿 Our Mission

Nature. For Everyone.

We believe meaningful experiences in nature should be accessible to anyone who can walk. At Join With Me, we create guided nature walks and outdoor experiences designed for children, parents, grandparents, and pets. Whether it's your first time stepping onto a trail or your hundredth, we make it easy, safe, and enjoyable to reconnect with the natural world. Because nature isn't meant for a few adventurers, it's meant for all of us.

Designed for Families

Simple, beginner-friendly nature walks that can be enjoyed by children, parents, and grandparents together.

🌿 Making Nature Accessible

🐶 Nature for Every Member of the Family

Story Details

" I recently had the privilege of joining the "Sunflower Hike" at Boisar with Join With Me, and it was an experience that exceeded all my expectations. From the moment I arrived, the founder, Raj Chheda, welcomed everyone with warmth and humility. It’s rare to meet someone so dedicated to creating such meaningful experiences in nature, and his presence truly set the tone for the day. The hike itself was a perfect blend of adventure and serenity. As we trekked through the lush landscape, I was enchanted by the hidden pond that we stumbled upon. We also explored a beautiful Shiv temple on the way, then came the banyan tree, to be around 150 years old, and stood as a silent guardian of the forest. Its sprawling roots and massive branches created a perfect spot for us to pause, relax, and soak in the beauty of nature. Of course, the highlight of the hike was the sunflower field itself. The golden blooms stretched as far as the eye could see, and it was a dream come true to be surrounded by so much vibrant beauty. It had been on my bucket list for years, and standing in the midst of the sunflowers, it was an emotional moment for me since the attachment i have with sunflowers on a personal level is beautiful. The "farm-to-table" experience was another highlight. The breakfast and lunch provided were fresh, wholesome, and absolutely delicious. You could feel the care and love that went into preparing each meal. Oh the lemOn juice"

Ruchika Agrawal

Happy Experiencer
